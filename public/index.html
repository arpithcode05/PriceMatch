About PriceMatch

A full-stack online price comparison prototype built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express and SQLite. It demonstrates product management, price comparison, price history and CSV export.

PriceMatch · Student prototype · Sample prices are illustrative and must be verified with retailers.
